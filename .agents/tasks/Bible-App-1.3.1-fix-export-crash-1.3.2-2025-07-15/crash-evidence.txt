automating GUI Export click requires the snap app to open its window, navigate to Studies,

=== CONCLUSION ===
Most likely root cause: font fix code runs synchronously at module level before GTK
initialises, mutating fonts.conf while fontconfig's internal state may be unset. When the
second BrowserWindow (sandbox: true) is created by renderInHiddenWindow() on Export click,
GTK/fontconfig re-reads fonts.conf and attempts to rebuild its cache directory. If the
XDG_CACHE_HOME/fontconfig directory does not exist or fontconfig 2.12.6 encounters a stale
internal state (from module-level mutation before GTK init), it crashes with SIGABRT/SIGSEGV.
